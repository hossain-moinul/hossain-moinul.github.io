Vision for Automated Tomato Harvesting

YOLOv5-Driven Decision Fusion Approach for Aiding Automated Tomato Harvesting: Detection, Maturity Classification, Peduncle Estimation and Picking Point Determination

Computer visionObject detectionDecision fusion
Image input
Fruit & maturityPeduncle location

picking point

Conceptual summary of the published perception pipeline.

The Question

Harvesting requires identifying a suitable fruit and locating where to pick it. Small peduncles, occlusion, and variable field conditions make these connected tasks challenging.

Approach

Two YOLOv5 models handle separate tasks: tomato detection with maturity classification, and peduncle localization. Their outputs are combined to identify candidate picking points at the centers of the peduncle boxes.

Findings & Next Steps

The tomato model reported 93.1% mAP@0.5. The selected peduncle model achieved 100% precision with 48.5% recall on the study’s test set, highlighting an important precision–recall trade-off.

Scope & Status

Evaluated on agricultural images. Integration with a physical harvesting system is a future direction.

Grounding Bengali Expressions in Images

Bengali Referring-Expression Grounding through Multilingual Adaptation of MDETR

Multimodal learningMDETRXLM-R

The Question

Given an image and a Bengali phrase describing an object, the model predicts a bounding box around that object. The project tests how a pretrained English grounding model can be adapted to Bengali with limited training data and GPU memory.

Approach

MDETR’s language encoder is replaced with XLM-R. Initial experiments train only the FeatureResizer that maps language features into the detector’s input space, while keeping the remaining compatible pretrained components frozen. English-only, Bengali-only, and bilingual pilot model training and validation has been checked.

Findings & Next Steps

The bilingual run with matched per-language training exposure improved validation grounding in both languages over the corresponding monolingual (English only and Bengali only) runs.

Understanding Bengali Symptom Descriptions

An Explainable Deep Learning Approach for Disease Detection and Medical Specialist Recommendation from Bengali Symptom Descriptions

Natural language processingTransformersExplainable AI

The Question

Bengali symptom descriptions contain natural-language information that differs from structured symptom checklists. This project investigates models that can classify such descriptions and make their predictions easier to inspect.

Approach

Deep-learning and pretrained transformer models classify Bengali symptom descriptions into disease categories. A fixed mapping connects each predicted disease to a specialist category. LIME and SHAP are used to inspect which words influence predictions.

Findings & Next Steps

The study compares classification performance across model families and examines whether the words highlighted by the explanations are consistent with the symptom descriptions.

Scope & Status

Experimental evaluation on Bengali symptom text. Clinical validation is a future direction.

Attention-enhanced Tomato Perception

An Improved YOLOv8 with Coordinate Attention for Tomato Maturity Detection and Peduncle Localization

Object detectionCoordinate attentionAgricultural AI

The Question

Tomato maturity detection and peduncle localization remain difficult under variable field conditions and partial occlusion.

Approach

The study adds coordinate attention to YOLOv8 architectures and evaluates tomato maturity detection and peduncle localization using field-collected images from Bangladesh.
